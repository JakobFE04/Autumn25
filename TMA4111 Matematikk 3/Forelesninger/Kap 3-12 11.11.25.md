## Enhetskulen:
$g:[0,2\pi)\times[0,\pu{\pi })\to \mathbb{R}^3$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 g(\theta,\phi)
 \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\cos \theta \sin \phi\\
\sin \theta \sin \phi \\
\cos \phi
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
I fysikk er det motsatt med $\theta$ og $\phi$ mens i matte er de sta og.

# Overflateareal
Likt som i bruke koordinattransformasjon til å regne dobbeltintegraler for komplekse funksjoner, så skal vi bruke koordinattransformasjoner til å regne overflateareal ved å ta koordinattransformasjon med 3 variable.

$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \int_{}^{}\int_{\Sigma}^{} dS \to \text{Flateelement}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies \int_{}^{}\int_{D}^{} |\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}| dx_{1}dx_{2} 
 \end{equation} 
$$

For vektorer i 3 dimensjoner:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \mathbb{R}^3\implies A=|v\times w |
 \end{equation} 
$$

## Enhetskulen:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
  |\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}| = \Bigg|\begin{array}{c}
\sin \theta \sin^2\phi \\
\cos \theta \sin^2\phi \\
\cos \phi \sin \phi
\end{array}\Bigg| = \sqrt{ \sin^2\theta \sin^4\phi+\cos^2\theta \sin^4\phi+\cos^2\phi\sin^2\phi } = \sqrt{ \sin^2\phi (\sin^2\phi +\cos^2\phi) }
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
  \implies |\sin \phi| = \sin \phi \ \ \ \phi:[0,\pi)
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies\int_{}^{}\int_{\Sigma}^{} dS = \int_{0}^{\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin \phi \ d\theta d\phi = 2\pi (-\cos \phi)|_{\phi=0}^{\phi=\pi} = 4\pi
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \text{Flateintegralet} = \int_{}^{}\int_{\Sigma}^{} f \ dS = \int_{}^{} \int_{D}^{} f(g(y)) |\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}|  \ dy
 \end{equation} 
$$

# Fluksintegralet
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \int_{}^{}\int_{\Sigma}^{}f \cdot dS=\int_{}^{}\int_{\Sigma}^{} f \cdot e_{n} \ dS = \int_{}^{}\int_{D}^{}f(g(y)) \frac{\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}}{|\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}|} \ |\frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}}| \ dy = \int_{}^{}\int_{D}^{}f(g(y)) \cdot \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{1}}\times \frac{\partial g}{\partial y_{2}} \ dy
 \end{equation} 
$$
Fluksintegral = "Fluksen" til vektorfeltet $f$ over $\Sigma$.

 \end{equation}
