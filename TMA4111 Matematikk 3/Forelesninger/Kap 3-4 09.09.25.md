##### Et viktig spesialtilfelle
![[09.09.25 Bilde nr 5.jpg]]
$f:\mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}$ $\nabla f:\mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}^2$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \nabla f=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{1}} \\
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{2}}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
slik at dersom $g=\nabla f$                       ($f:\mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}      \ \ \ \ g:\mathbb{R}^n\to \mathbb{R}^n$ )

Kalles g et gradientfelt


$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f(x)=\frac{1}{|x|} =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ x_{1}^2+x_{2}^2 +x_{3}^2}}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \nabla f=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\frac{-x_{1}}{(x_{1}^2+x_{2}^2+x_{3}^2)^3} \\
\frac{-x_{2}}{(x_{1}^2+x_{2}^2+x_{3}^2)^3} \\
\frac{-x_{3}}{(x_{1}^2+x_{2}^2+x_{3}^2)^3}
\end{array}\Bigg] =-\frac{1}{|x|^3}\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
=> Gravitasjonskraft og kraft fra punktladning er konservative

### Felt vs kraft
Gravitasjonskraft (matematisk funksjon som kan beskrives som masse piler i rommet)
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 F=\frac{Gm_{1}m_{2}}{|x|^3}\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Gravitasjonsfelt (En tenkt kraft på et objekt med gitt masse m)
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 F=\frac{Gm}{|x|^3}\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Elektrisk felt
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 E=\frac{q}{4\pi \epsilon|x|^3}\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=-\frac{q}{4\pi \epsilon_{0}}\cdot \frac{1}{|x|} 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Spenning: $E=-\nabla V$

#### Mer om konservative
Pendellikningen

Newtons 2. lov og lengde på masse = 1
$$
 \begin{equation} 
\ddot{\theta} +\sin \theta=0 \ \ \ \\ | \cdot \dot{\theta}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \dot{\theta} \ddot{\theta}+ \dot{\theta}\sin \theta=0 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \frac{1}{2}(\dot{\theta})^2- \cos \theta=0
 \end{equation} 
$$
Som blir bevaring av mekanisk energi loven^^ (Potensiell = T, Kinetisk = V)

$q=\theta$ & $p=\dot{\theta}$ => $\dot{q}=p$ & $\dot{p}=-\sin q$

#### Lagrange
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 L(q,p)=T -V = \frac{1}{2}p^2+\cos q
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\dot{q} \\
\dot{p}
\end{array}\Bigg] =\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
p \\
-\sin q
\end{array}\Bigg]=\nabla L
 \end{equation} 
$$
Noe som går "langs" vektorfeltet, kalles strømlinjer der $\dot{x}=f(x)$

#### Hamilton funksjoner
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 H(q,p)=T+V 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \frac{d}{dt} H(q(t),p(t))=\frac{\partial H}{\partial q}\dot{q}+\frac{\partial H}{\partial p}\dot{p\implies H=\frac{1}{2}p^2-\cos q}
 \end{equation} 
$$

 W=\int_{x(a)}^{x(b)}F(x(t)) \cdot T(t)dl=\int_{a}^{b}F(x(t))\cdot \dot{x}(t)dt
 \end{equation} 
$$
