### Sist: Areal av vegg ($\Gamma$ i arealtegningen)
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \int_{\Gamma}^{}f \ ds=\int_{a}^{b}f(x(t))1\dot{x}(t)|  \ dt 
 \end{equation} 
$$

## I dag: Volumet av huset
R2: Omdreiningslegemer

$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A=\pi f^2(x) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\pi \int_{a}^{b}f^2(x) \ dx 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Vi integrer en arealfunksjon for å finne volum!

Volumet til huset skrives:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \int_{}^{}\int_{\Omega}^{} f
 \end{equation} 
$$
Dobbeltintegral

#### Eks:
$\Omega=[0,1]\times[0,1]$ (Kartesisk produkt)

$f(x)=x_{1}x_{2}$

Arealet til tversnittet:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A(x_{1})=\int_{0}^{1}f(x_{1},x_{2}) \ dx_{2} 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{1}x_{1}x_{2} \ dx_{2} \ dx_{1}= \frac{1}{4}
 \end{equation} 
$$

#### Eks 2:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f(x)=x_{1}x_{2} 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A(x_{1})= \int_{0}^{x_{1}}f(x) \ dx_{2}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{x_{1}}x_{1}x_{2}  \ dx_{2} \ dx_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies \int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{2}x_{1} x_{2}^2 |_{0}^{x_{1}} \ dx_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}x_{1}^3 \ dx_{1} = \frac{1}{8}
 \end{equation} 
$$

Motsatt vei:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A(x_{2})=\int_{x_{2}}^{1} x_{1}x_{2} \ dx_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\int_{0}^{1}\int_{x_{2}}^{1}x_{1} x_{2} \ dx_{1} \ dx_{2}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies\int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{2} x_{1}^2 x_{2} |_{x_{2}}^1 \ dx_{2}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies  \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1} x_{2}^2-x_{2}^3 \ dx_{2} = \frac{1}{8}
 \end{equation} 
$$


#### Mer eks:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A(x_{1})=\int_{0}^{1+x_{1}} x_{1}x_{2} \ dx_{2}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\int_{0}^{1}\int_{0}^{1+x_{1}}x_{1} x_{2} \ dx_{2} \ dx_{1}

$$

 \begin{equation} 
 A(x_{2})=\int_{0}^{1}x_{1}x_{2} \ dx_{1} \  + \int_{x_{2}-1}^{1}x_{1} x_{2} \ dx_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 V=\int_{0}^{1}\left( \int_{0}^{1} x_{1}x_{2} \ dx_{1} + \int_{x_{2}-1}^{1}x_{1} x_{2} dx_{1} \right) dx_{2} 
 \end{equation} 
$$



#### Mer mer eks:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f(x)=1 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 x_{1}=x_{2}^2 \ \\ \ \ \ \& \ \ \\ \ \ x_{2}=x_{1}^2 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 A=\int_{0}^{1} \int_{x_{1}^2}^{\sqrt{ x_{1} }} dx_{2} dx_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{ x_{1} }-x_{1}^2  \ dx_{1} = \frac{1}{3}
 \end{equation} 
$$

#### Mer mer mer eks
![[Pasted image 20250923105003.png]]
Vi må ha parametrisering for $\Gamma$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
  \Gamma= \{y(t), t\epsilon [0,1]\}
 \end{equation} 
$$
Punktene mellom linjen blir da
$$
 \begin{equation} 
  \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1}^2 \\
x_{1}
\end{array}\Bigg] \ \ \ \& \ \ \ \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_1^2
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
  y(t)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
x_{1}^2
\end{array}\Bigg] + t \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1}^2-x_{1} \\
x_{1} - x_{1}^2
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 y'(t)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1}^2-x_{1} \\
x_{1}-x_{1}^2
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$$$
 \begin{equation} 
 |y'(t)| = \sqrt{ (x_{1}^2-x_{1})^2+(x_{1}-x_{1}^2)^2 } = \sqrt{ 2 }(x_{1}-x_{1}^2)
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies A(x_{1})=\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{ 2 }(x_{1}-x_{1}^2) \ dt = \sqrt{ 2 }(x_{1}-x_{1}^2) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \implies V = \sqrt{ 2 } \int_{0}^{1} (x_{1}-x_{1}^2) \sqrt{ 2 } \ dx_{1}= \frac{1}{3}
 \end{equation} 
$$
(Må gange med $\sqrt{ 2 }$ for å kompansere for at linjen er nå $\sqrt{ 2 }$ lang)
