# Mere Flervariabel Kalkulus
![[26.08.25 nr 1.png]]
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 y=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)
 \end{equation} 
$$

$f:\mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}$ har tangentplan

![[26.08.25 nr 2.png]]
Da har tangentplanet også formelen:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 y=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Eks:
$f(x)=x_{1}^2+x_{1}x_{2}+1$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f'(x)=[2x_{1}+x_{2},x_{1}] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Tangentplan for $$
 \begin{equation} 
 a=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
3 \\
1
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} \implies y=13+[7,1]\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1}-3 \\
x_{2}-1
\end{array}\Bigg]
$$

## Noe mer nyttig: Kjerneregelen
Eks: $f(x)=ax_{1}+bx_{2}$

![[26.08.25 nr 3.png]]
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 g(s)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
y_{1} \\
y_{2} 
\end{array}\Bigg] +s\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\cos \theta \\
\sin \theta
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(s)=f(g(s)) =a(y_{1}+s\cos \theta)+b(y_{2}+s \sin \theta)
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h'(s)=a\cos \theta+b\sin \theta
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f'(x)=[a,b] \ \ \ \ g'(s)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\cos \theta \\
\sin \theta
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
### Tilfeldig? Neppe
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(x)=f(g(x)) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 g:\mathbb{R}^m\to \mathbb{R}^n \ \ \ \ \  f:\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h:\mathbb{R}^m\to \mathbb{R}^n 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x) 
 \end{equation} 
$$

Må huske Jacobimatrisen:
$$
  \begin{equation} 
   f'(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
f'_{1}(x) \\
f'_{2}(x) \\
\vdots \\
f'_{n}(x)
\end{array}\Bigg] =\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{1}},\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{m}} \\
\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{1}},\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{m}} \\
\vdots \\ 
\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x},\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x_{m}}
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$

Eks:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
(x_{1}+x_{2})^2 \\
x_{2}
\end{array}\Bigg]  \ \ \ \\ g(z)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
-z_{1}-z_{2} \\
z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
som vil si $f: \mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}^3$ og $g:\mathbb{R}^3\to R^2$

$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(z)=f(g(z)) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 g'(z)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
-1 & -1 & 0 \\
1 & 1 & 1
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f'(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
1 & 0 \\
2(x_{1}+x_{2}) & 2(x_{1}+x_{2}) \\
0 & 1
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(z)=f'(g(z))g'(z)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
1 & 0 \\
2(z_{3}) & 2(z_{3}) \\
0 & 1
\end{array}\Bigg] \times \Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
-1 & -1 & 0 \\
1 & 1 & 1 
\end{array}\Bigg]=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
-1 & -1 & 1 \\
0  & 0 & 2z_{3}\\
1 & 1 & 1
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
