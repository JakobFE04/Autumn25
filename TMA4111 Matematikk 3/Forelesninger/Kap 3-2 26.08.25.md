### Tilfeldig? Neppe
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(x)=f(g(x)) 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 g:\mathbb{R}^m\to \mathbb{R}^n \ \ \ \ \  f:\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^p
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h:\mathbb{R}^m\to \mathbb{R}^n 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x) 
 \end{equation} 
$$

Må huske Jacobimatrisen:
$$
  \begin{equation} 
   f'(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
f'_{1}(x) \\
f'_{2}(x) \\
\vdots \\
f'_{n}(x)
\end{array}\Bigg] =\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{1}},\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{1}}{\partial x_{m}} \\
\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{1}},\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{2}}{\partial x_{m}} \\
\vdots \\ 
\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x},\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x_{2}}\dots\frac{\partial f_{n}}{\partial x_{m}}
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$

Eks:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
x_{1} \\
(x_{1}+x_{2})^2 \\
x_{2}
\end{array}\Bigg]  \ \ \ \\ g(z)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
-z_{1}-z_{2} \\
z_{1}+z_{2}+z_{3}
\end{array}\Bigg]
 \end{equation} 
$$
som vil si $f: \mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}^3$ og $g:\mathbb{R}^3\to R^2$

$$
 \begin{equation} 
 h(z)=f(g(z)) 
 \end{equation}

 \end{equation}

# Høyere ordens deriverte:
Eks: $f(x)=x_{1}^2+x_{1}x_{2}+2x_{2}^2+x_{1}$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f'(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
2x_{1}+x_{2}+1 & +x_{1}+4x_{2}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f''(x)=\text{Egentilig en tredke ordens tensord} 
 \end{equation} 
$$
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f''(x)= \text{Men vi dropper det og skriver noe annet}
 \end{equation} 
$$

"Jeg tror verden hadde vært et bedre sted om alle kunne tensord regning, men det er kanskje fordi jeg ikke kan det selv." - Nome 2025

Hessematrisen: (kun for skalarfelt)
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 H(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x_{1}^2} & \frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x_{1},\partial x_{2}} \\
\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x_{2},\partial x_{1}} & \frac{\partial^2f}{\partial x_{2}^2}
\end{array}\Bigg] 
 \end{equation} 
$$
Kommer aldri til å dobbelt derivere noe annet enn skalarfelt:
$$
 \begin{equation} 
 \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{1}} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_{2}} =\text{Deriverte med hensyn på }x_{2}\text{ også derivert med }x_{1}
 \end{equation} 
$$

$$
 \begin{equation} 
 f''(x)=\Bigg[\begin{array}{c}
2 & 1 \\
1 & 4
\end{array}\Bigg] =\text{Symmetrisk <3} \implies \text{Spektralteoremet: Ortogonalt diagonaliserbar}
 \end{equation} 
$$
Som vil si at fortegnene på egenverdiene klassifiserer kritiske punkt (Om H ikke er singulær).
- At vi kan finne ut om punktet er et maks eller minimum punkt.
